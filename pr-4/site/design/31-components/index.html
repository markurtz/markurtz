    31 · Components¶

    Status: Locked (Component Workbench final 2026-11-21; page-header, header and share-button changes through 2026-11-23). Reference markup and CSS: the mock's src/components.mjs and src/styles/site.css; earlier workbench source in components-workbench/ with section line numbers in component-decisions.md (sources).

    Site header¶

    • 68 px bar. Lockup left (monogram tile + "markurtz" with a teal k). Links right: Professional, Workshop, Studio, Blog, About, in muted ink, 0.95 rem, weight 500.
    • Current section: ink, weight 600, a 2 px teal line along the bottom edge (inset 0 -2px 0). Replaces the earlier pill nav.
    • Then the theme button. No Get in touch (removed 2026-11-23).
    • Under 760 px: links fold into a menu button that opens a full-width panel.
    • Gains a hairline border once the page scrolls. On the home page it is transparent over the hero until scroll.

    Footer¶

    Side by side on the page background with a hairline on top (not navy). Left: invitation and a small primary Get in touch. Right: Uses · Colophon · Privacy · Clients, then the profile icon buttons with names and tooltips. Base row: small lockup left, "© 2026 Mark Kurtz · Photos CC BY-NC · Code on GitHub" right.

    Page header (.ph)¶

    On the page background, no box. Min height clamp(300px, 30vw, 440px). Text column up to 54% (660 px), gap 14 px: role line (Geist Mono 500, clamp(.82rem, …, .95rem), tracking .04em, muted), path title (.ph-title; parent part .ph-up muted 700, separator .ph-sep in --color-line-strong), lede (.ph-lede, display font, muted), meta, actions. Art (.ph-art) right-aligned at ~64% width, fading on all four edges with a mask; subject in ink, network slate and teal. Below 1024 px the art becomes a faded band above the text.

    List toolbar¶

    One row: segmented switch (sunken track, segments with mono counts, current segment raised), then search (~260 px, icon inside, placeholder per page) and the year menu, right-aligned; wraps below on narrow screens. Blog: picking a section reveals its sub-label segments. Client gallery: All/New switch, four dropdown checkbox menus with counts, "Select all N".

    Pinned band (.pin-band)¶

    Teal-tinted sunken band with a star label (Pinned / Featured / Favorites), 24 px above the toolbar, holding small cards in an auto-fill grid (250 px min).

    Year rail (.rail, .rail-y)¶

    Two columns: a 96 px rail with the year as a large muted numeral, then that year's cards. No headings over hairlines. On phones the year sits above its cards. Each group has id="y-YYYY".

    Jump bar¶

    Above every timeline with three or more stops: hairline track; teal dots sized by item count; mono labels; the stop in view highlighted; filtered-away stops faded. Phones show the two end labels and the current one.

    Sizes per item: compact, standard, featured (P1.2). Hover on every card: border turns teal, no lift.

    Photo layouts¶

    • Justified rows (<JustifiedRows>): rows filled edge to edge at a target height (about 280 px desktop, 180 px phone), photos never cropped or retouched ("untouched", Locked), 3 px gaps.
    • Photo timeline (<PhotoTimeline>): the year-rail look for moments; rail labels in the unit that fits the span (clock times, "Day 3", months); a range strip on top places each moment.

    Item actions¶

    Three 36 px round buttons (Copy link, Embed, More) on a translucent dark chip at the top right of each photo or film. J1 icons in white. Toast at the bottom centre, role="status", 3 s, with an optional "Change" action. Embed popover: snippet in a mono read-only field and Copy. More: menu with 2 to 3 items (04 § 6).

    Viewer (dialog.pv)¶

    Always dark (navy-950 at 96%). The photo fills the dialog. 44 px round previous and next at the sides; Copy link, Embed, More and Close top right; bottom gradient with caption, mono "4 / 6" and a filmstrip of thumbnails (current outlined in teal). Strip hidden under 600 px. Films play in place with native controls kept clear of the strip.

    Video¶

    Part Spec
    Card No play button; a mono "▶ 0:12" tag top left; muted preview loops on hover (loaded on first hover); posters on touch. Exception: /studio shows a still poster with play mark and length, no hover.
    Cinema band .cin Full-width navy-950 band; film up to 1100 px at 16:9 with native controls and poster; path title and "Place · Month Year · Film and N photos" under it in light text
    Talk embed Thumbnail with play mark; on press, youtube-nocookie.com iframe in place

    Forms¶

    Floating labels (Locked). Labels always present; hints under the field; errors in words under the field in --color-danger with an icon; required fields marked in words. Checkboxes as rows with a muted explanation ("Download · Save the full-size photos"). Dialog sheet for share options.

    Fact panel¶

    Two-column definition list in a hairline card: label (muted, mono) and value. Empty rows are not rendered.

    Edge cards¶

    404, link-expired and "Open your photos" use a centred card (max ~560 px) with the edge art on top (960 × 440) at 12 px radius, then role line, title, text and actions.
