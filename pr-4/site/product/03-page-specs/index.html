    Shared parts¶

    Page header (PG-1)¶

    Every inner page opens with the banner-style header, on the page background with no box, outline or panel (Locked 2026-11-22). Text is left-aligned with the site header's lockup.

    Top to bottom:

    1. Role line in Geist Mono: three words separated by " · ". The first word is the home line's best fit (Researcher, Engineer or Creator), then two role nouns (Locked 2026-11-23, table below).
    2. Title in Inter 800, about 2.1 to 3.6 rem. On sub-pages the title is a path title: "Professional / Software", where "Professional" links up and the slash is muted (option B, Locked 2026-11-23). Studio work and photo pages use it too ("Studio / Iceland / Photo 2 of 3").
    3. Lede: one muted Inter sentence.
    4. Meta (optional): smaller muted line, e.g. "date · Mark Kurtz · 6 min read".
    5. Actions (optional): the page's button pair.
    6. Art: the page's marker-drawn art (33), in the right column only, on the page background, beside the text: the title, lede and buttons fill the left side, and the art is never full width or behind them. On tablets and phones it becomes a band above the text. The teal marker draws the subject live once; the animation stops as soon as the subject locks in place in ink (white in dark mode). It does not loop.

    Pages with their own tops: the home hero, Studio work pages with a film (cinema band), 404, link-expired, and the client "Open your photos" card.

    The shared list template (PG-2)¶

    Software, Research, Speaking, Builds, Designs, Code, Studio and Blog are one component that adapts its cards to the genre.

    Part Behaviour
    Pinned band Directly under the page header, then 24 px of space before the toolbar. Soft teal-tinted band, label with a star ("Pinned"; "Featured" on Speaking; "Favorites" on Studio). Always the small icon card: icon tile or 64 px thumb, eyebrow, two-line title, no image on Professional and Workshop. Every pinned item also appears at full size in the timeline.
    Toolbar One segmented switch with a count per option in mono ("All 10 · Active 6 …"), then search (about 260 px) and the year menu on the same row; they wrap under it on narrow screens. Then a count line ("N posts"), except on Studio.
    Jump links A quiet row of small mono "Jump to" year links beside the count (days on Studio work pages). The year in view is in ink with a teal underline. Replaces the clickable year timeline with dots, retired on all 11 pages (2026-11-24). Links only navigate; the year menu stays the filter.
    Year rail Every item newest first. Each year is a large muted numeral in a 96 px left rail beside that year's cards, no year headings. From 900 px up the year stays pinned while its cards scroll by. Below that the year stacks above its cards. Year groups carry id="y-YYYY".
    In progress An "In progress" or "Coming soon" badge on the card, in the timeline and in the pinned band. No separate page.
    Cards Per genre: repo card (Software), research entry (Research), talk card (Speaking), framed photo card (Workshop, Blog), title-on-photo card (Studio). Sizes per item: compact, standard, featured (31).
    Empty result "No posts match that. Try a different search, or clear all filters." (wording per page) with a Clear filters button.
    Blog strip "Written between projects" with "See all posts", using Home's card, four across, posts from this page's section first. Studio uses "What I shoot with" instead.

    Home card (PG-3)¶

    One card for every home row and every blog strip: framed, 16:9 media, a small pill top-left of the media for type cues (language, Paper/Patent, In progress, Film, source ↗), an eyebrow (one line, ellipsis) and a title clamped to two lines (always reserving two). No description. Four across from 960 px, two from 640 px, one below.

    Contact popup (PG-4)¶

    Opened by the footer's Get in touch, by every section header's primary button, and by #contact.

    • Title "Say hi". Intro: "Whether it's a project, a talk or a question about a build, I'd like to hear about it. I reply to every message [Held: reply timing, or drop it]."
    • Fields: Name, Email, Message (floating labels), a hidden trap field, a privacy-friendly challenge.
    • Button "Send message". Note: "Protected by a privacy-friendly spam check. No tracking."
    • Success replaces the form with a thank-you line; errors are in words next to the field.
    • No list of reasons to write, no offer of commissions or shoots.

    Footer (PG-5)¶

    • Left: "Want to build something together?" and a small primary Get in touch.
    • Right: Uses · Colophon · Privacy · Clients, then profile icon buttons with accessible names and tooltips: GitHub, LinkedIn, Google Scholar, X, Instagram, Reddit, Medium, alphaXiv, RSS.
    • Base row: small lockup; "© 2026 Mark Kurtz · Photos CC BY-NC · Code on GitHub".
